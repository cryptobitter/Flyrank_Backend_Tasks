## 1. Deliverable

### The One-Paragraph Proof Statement
> I build robust, containerized Python backend REST APIs with clean repository patterns and relational database integrations that run reliably in production. I am targeting an Engineering Lead or Technical Founder at an early-stage B2B SaaS startup looking for an execution-focused backend engineer. The single action I want them to take after evaluating my build is to book a 15-minute technical walkthrough call on my calendar.

---

### The One-Line Why
> A CV or LinkedIn bullet point can claim 'Python & SQL proficiency', but only a live, containerized service with clean repository architecture proves I write maintainable, production-ready backend code without hand-holding.

## 3. The Narrowing Interview (Thinking Partner Dialogue)

To satisfy the brief (*Use AI as a thinking partner, not an author: have it interview you with sharp questions until the claim is genuinely narrow and true*), this statement was refined through four pressure-test rounds:

### Round 1: The Claim
- **Interviewer**: *What do you actually want to be hired to do? Don't give me a list of languages or buzzwords like 'Full Stack Developer with AI interests'.*
- **Push-back**: *If you say 'Full-stack developer', you're competing with millions of generalists. What is the single layer where your code is strongest and cleanest?*
- **Refinement**: Focus on **Backend Architecture** — designing clean APIs, structuring database queries, containerization, and repository separation.

### Round 2: The Target Person
- **Interviewer**: *Who is the exact human being on the other side of the screen who has the power to hire you?*
- **Push-back**: *'Recruiters' don't read backend code; they scan keywords. Who actually cares that you used a repository pattern rather than dumping SQL queries into route handlers?*
- **Refinement**: **An Engineering Lead or Technical Founder at an early-stage B2B SaaS company**. They feel the direct pain of poorly structured code and technical debt.

### Round 3: The Single Action
- **Interviewer**: *When they land on your work and see the proof, what is the single next step you want them to take?*
- **Push-back**: *'Email me' is passive and easily postponed. 'Hire me' is too big of a leap. What is the lowest-friction, highest-value conversion step?*
- **Refinement**: **Book a 15-minute technical walkthrough call**. It gives you the floor to demonstrate the system live.

### Round 4: The One-Line Why
- **Interviewer**: *Why can't you just link your LinkedIn or email your CV?*
- **Refinement**: *Anyone can list FastAPI, Docker, and PostgreSQL on a resume. None of that shows if your code is modular, testable, or clean. The portfolio is the live, working evidence.*
